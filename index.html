career-ops

A personal job-search tool used by one person, K Aditya Satya Prakash, to organise their own job applications.

What it does

  • Reads LinkedIn job-alert emails in the owner's own Gmail inbox (read-only access).
  • Extracts the job links from those emails and adds them to a local list on the owner's computer.
  • Never sends, deletes, or changes any email, and is not offered to other users.
